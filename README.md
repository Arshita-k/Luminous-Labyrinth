# Welcome to Luminous Labyrinth: Pac-Man's Quest
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/522c3cd1-6755-4ed8-a065-114af3282f1e)


## DESCRIPTION:
Welcome to the next level of Pac-Man! Our Luminous Labyrinth game isn't just a nostalgic throwback; it's a reinvention of the classic arcade masterpiece. Immerse yourself in a world where strategic decision points, dynamic environmental changes, and thrilling risk-reward mechanics redefine the Pac-Man experience.
Embark on a journey where reaching the four corners of the maze isn't just about points; it's a strategic choice that will test your critical thinking and logical reasoning. Witness the maze transform with darkness, forcing you to adapt your gameplay and adding a visual twist that keeps you on the edge.
But the innovation doesn't stop there – devour a cherry, and ghosts turn pink for a limited time. Will you seize the opportunity to rack up points or strategically evade during regular phases? The choice is yours, adding an exhilarating layer of decision-making to every move.
We've also reimagined the frustration of losing lives. No more tedious restarts after each life; experience a game that respects your progress, bringing you back to the starting point only after completing all three lives.


## NOVELTY:
Our Luminous Labyrinth, the 3D Pac-Man quest, sets itself apart by seamlessly merging nostalgic elements with cutting-edge features, distinguishing it from classic 2D Pac-Man games and other 3D titles. Introducing strategic decision points, such as reaching four corners to consume unique lamps, challenges logical reasoning and promotes thoughtful planning for maximizing effectiveness.

* The innovative use of darkness, a consequence of eating lamps, introduces a layer of complexity seldom seen in Pac-Man games. This environmental twist impacts aesthetics and requires players to adapt their strategies, creating a dynamic and immersive gaming experience.
* Incorporating a cherry power-up that turns ghosts pink for a limited time introduces a thrilling risk-reward dynamic. Players face decisions about when to evade ghosts during regular phases or capitalize on their temporary vulnerability for high-scoring opportunities, setting our game apart with nuanced decision-making.
* The return-to-starting-point mechanic, activated after losing all three lives, enhances player satisfaction and minimizes frustration, creating a more enjoyable gaming experience. Coupled with a user-friendly UI, the game ensures easy navigation and understanding, making it accessible to casual and seasoned gamers.
  
The Luminous Labyrinth game harmoniously blends classic gameplay, strategic depth, and visual innovation, providing players with a captivating and unparalleled experience in the Pac-Man universe.


## TECHNICAL DETAILS:
In crafting your 3D Pac-Man game using Unity, several essential engine features were harnessed to deliver a compelling gaming experience. Unity's scene management system was pivotal in organizing various game states, allowing seamless transitions between levels, menus, and other elements. The game's visually appealing 3D environment was brought to life through Unity's powerful graphics engine, encompassing the rendering of textures and materials and the implementation of shaders to achieve effects like darkness. The physics system within Unity contributed to the intricacies of Pac-Man's movement and collision detection with dots, lamps, ghosts, and maze walls.
Scripting played a crucial role in defining the game's logic and behavior. C# scripts handled Pac-Man, ghost movements, scoring mechanisms, and various in-game interactions. Unity's animation system further enhanced the gaming experience, facilitating character animations such as Pac-Man's eating motion and the dynamic color changes of the ghosts.
The user-friendly and compatible UI, a standout feature of your game, was crafted using Unity's UI system. Canvas elements, Text components, and buttons were skillfully utilized to display scores, lives, and other vital game information. Visual effects, such as the disappearance of ghosts, were achieved through Unity's particle system, adding a layer of dynamism to the gaming environment.
The game's aesthetics were enriched by importing and integrating 3D models, textures, and audio assets. Unity's flexibility in this regard allowed for the seamless integration of assets from the Unity Asset Store and other external sources, contributing to the game's overall appeal. Timed events, such as the 10-second color change for ghosts after eating a cherry, were implemented using Unity's coroutines.
Ensuring broad accessibility, Unity's tools for mobile development were leveraged to optimize and build the game for various devices. This comprehensive utilization of Unity's functionalities allowed for the creating a dynamic and engaging 3D Pac-Man game that successfully inculcates classic gameplay elements with innovative features and offers players a strategic, immersive, and thoroughly enjoyable gaming experience.
